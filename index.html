We explore websites and web technologies. This exploration may focus on the technology itself or it may examine how the technology interacts with and influences the real world. We decide on each topic together and each member conducts further research on topics of personal interest. When this research is shared with the group, each piece of content is turned into a small website. These websites containing the content are linked in a certain way★ to the website you are currently viewing. The websites created by each member as they explore their topics may blend with, clash with, or be reborn into new forms alongside the websites of other members. The name of this study group "t/t" originated from the HTML elements <table></table>. In the past, entire websites were sometimes structured as <table>.▲ On these tables, various stories are exchanged, and many things are added and removed.
